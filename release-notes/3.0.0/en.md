Swiss Bitcoin Pay becomes **sattle.me**: a new app, rebuilt from the ground up. Your account comes along in a few steps, with your funds, your settings and your history: open the app and follow the guide.

### 🛒 Get paid

- Three entry modes at the Point of Sale: **Amount**, **Items** or **Scan**, which adds your items to the basket by their barcode.
- One QR code to be paid over Lightning or on-chain.
- Your customer can also **tap their BoltCard**, or pay on their phone the payment request you share with them.
- Every sale followed live: confirming, paid, underpaid with what is left to pay, or expired.
- **Till mode**: after 5 minutes without activity, the app goes back to the Point of Sale and locks the rest of your account.

### 🔑 Your funds, your keys

- **Non-custodial** wallet: sattle.me never holds your funds or your keys.
- Your account opens with a **passkey**, asked for before every payment you send.
- **12 recovery words**, and a **rescue mode** that opens your account with them alone to move your funds to safety.
- Send to a Lightning address, a Lightning invoice, an LNURL link, a bitcoin address or a Spark address, with the fees shown before sending.

### 💱 Bitcoin and currencies

- Choose in **Split** the share of your takings to convert: the app offers to sell it, and Mt Pelerin pays the amount into your bank account.

### 🧾 Invoices and payment links

- Invoices with VAT, numbered in sequence, PDF in seven languages, to send and remind by email.
- Every invoice has its payment link: your customer opens it in their browser and pays with their wallet.

### 👥 Items and team

- Your items with photo and price, end-to-end encrypted.
- Invite your employees with a link or a QR code: they take payments and see their sales, with no access to your funds.

### 📊 Tracking and accounting

- In **Wallet**: your balance, what is **To follow up** and all your transactions, with the employee who took each sale.
- Export your transactions as PDF or CSV.

### 🤖 Online and with AI

- Plugins and guides for WooCommerce, PrestaShop, Magento, Wix, pretix and other online shops, and libraries for developers.
- Your **AI assistant** (Claude, ChatGPT…) makes your invoices and exports your transactions, as far as you allow it. You remove it whenever you like, and it never spends anything.

### 💳 NFC cards

- Program BoltCards with your phone, with limits and a choice of the merchants paid without confirmation.
- **Where to pay with your card**: the map of shops that accept Lightning contactless payments.

### 🔒 Privacy and comfort

- Settings, items, team and sales end-to-end encrypted on Nostr relays, which you can replace with your own.
- Biometric lock, light or dark theme, seven languages.
- No crash report sent without your consent.
